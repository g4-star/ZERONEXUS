## 🌐 About ZeroNexus

**ZeroNexus** is a cybersecurity community and innovation platform designed to showcase cybersecurity teams, projects, research, portfolios, and collaborative learning.

The platform provides a central hub where cybersecurity enthusiasts, students, developers, and security professionals can share knowledge, demonstrate projects, and collaborate on security-focused initiatives.

ZeroNexus focuses on:

- 🔐 Cybersecurity awareness
- 🛡️ Security research
- 💻 Technical projects
- 👥 Cybersecurity teams and communities
- 📚 Continuous learning
- 🚀 Innovation in technology

---
